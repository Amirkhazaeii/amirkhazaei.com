I build virtual worlds that behave like real ones, and use them to understand places, machines and the people who work with them.

My research sits between computer graphics, simulation and AI. I make digital twins of real environments, from national parks to industrial plants, and build interactive systems that let people explore and question them.

A growing part of my work is physical AI: training and evaluating robots in physically grounded simulation before they touch the real world. Across projects, the common thread is fidelity. A twin is only useful if its light, physics and behaviour can be trusted.
